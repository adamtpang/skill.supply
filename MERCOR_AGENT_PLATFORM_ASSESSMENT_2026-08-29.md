# Mercor Agent Platform assessment

Checked 2026-08-29 against Mercor's official Ashby feed, current careers page,
current independent reporting, current employee-review evidence, Adam's
canonical profile, and the S-tier target contract.

## Verdict

Reject [Fullstack Software Engineer, Agent Platform](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/mercor/3750ee8a-64d1-46ac-b4f9-6a823f949034)
from S-tier pursuit.

The role is one of Adam's strongest mechanical matches and Mercor has ample
hiring capacity. Those strengths cannot average away a failed people gate.
Current independent reporting describes a 996 norm, compressed and unrealistic
timelines, and alleged late-night attendance monitoring. Mercor disputed the
monitoring allegation but its spokesperson confirmed that the company expects
employees to work hard at customer pace and said the intensity may not suit
everyone. A current MTS review separately reports political management.

Do not apply as a dream-job target. Preserve it only as a deliberate
high-compensation income override if Adam explicitly accepts the workload and
culture risk.

## People-axis evidence

The direct evidence is strong enough to reject without waiting for an interview:

- Forbes interviewed more than a dozen former employees and reports that a 9
  a.m. to 9 p.m., six-day schedule is commonly described inside Mercor.
- Former employees reported fast-changing scopes and compressed, unrealistic
  timelines.
- Two former employees alleged that a senior executive tracked who was absent
  at 9 p.m. Mercor's spokesperson denied that monitoring occurred.
- The spokesperson did not describe a sustainable alternative cadence. Mercor
  said it does not mandate hours, expects employees to match customer pace, and
  acknowledged that the intensity might not suit everyone.
- Forbes reports an internal survey asking employees who lowered the bar, with
  responses visible to three top leaders.
- A current anonymous MTS review praises smart colleagues and development speed
  but reports political management. Glassdoor's overall 4.2 score is dominated
  by contractor and expert reviews, so it cannot override employee-specific
  leadership evidence for this engineering role.

This resembles the Cognition rejection. A large problem and high compensation
do not repair normalized extreme hours.
