# Pylon Software Engineer assessment

Checked 2026-08-29 against Pylon's current official role, official team
material, and current first-party recruiting statements.

## Verdict

Reject [Software Engineer](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/pylon-labs/edba2728-f620-49f8-b18c-7a4294fe08af)
from the S-tier campaign.

The role is structurally attractive. It has no stated years floor, uses React,
Go, GraphQL, and AWS, lists a $180,000 to $300,000 base band plus equity, and
works on a product close to Adam's customer-support and agent-workflow
experience. Pylon also reports more than 1,500 customers, $51 million raised,
and a team of more than 100 people.

The people gate fails. Current public recruiting describes the team as `happy
grinders` and says it `famously works hard`. The founders' own company story
describes a 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., six-day schedule and working almost all the time.
The live role advertises both lunch and dinner in the office. These signals are
too direct and consistent to treat as a temporary launch exception.
