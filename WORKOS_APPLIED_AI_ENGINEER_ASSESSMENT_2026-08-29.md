## Company problem and budget hypotheses

Treat these as hypotheses to test, not claims about WorkOS's private roadmap:

1. As WorkOS customers add agents, identity, delegated permissions, credential
   scope, human gates, and auditability become product requirements rather than
   internal implementation details.
2. The Applied AI team likely needs a reusable substrate that lets a small team
   ship many agents without multiplying silent failures, inconsistent identity,
   and bespoke observability.
3. Customer-facing ask.workos.com and support bots likely make answer quality,
   evidence visibility, escalation, and verified resolution more important than
   raw demo speed.
4. The approved salary band, equity, Series C, valuation, current hiring volume,
   and named large customers indicate a real budget for scarce agent-product
   engineers.

5. Is this opening new headcount, replacement, or a level expansion?
6. Which customer-facing or internal product and measurable outcome would Adam

7. Does WorkOS require residence in the US or Canada before the start date, and
   what relocation timing is acceptable?
8. What base, equity, and level map to Adam's evidence and 28 to 29 supportable
   engineering months?

Reject S-tier certification if the direct manager remains unknown through the
loop, recurring uncontrolled nights or weekends are normal, incident work has
no recovery, the role requires an unannounced seniority bar, or relocation
cannot clear the role's US and Canada location constraint.
