# Sola Fullstack role assessment

Checked 2026-08-29 against Sola's current Ashby role, company materials,
current leadership and engineer accounts, Adam's canonical profile, and the
rendered application form.

## Verdict

Advance [Software Engineer, Fullstack](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/sola/0c859bfa-c3e1-429d-876d-0542f5358c17)
as the strongest current low-friction application investigation.

The role is a direct match for Adam's TypeScript, React, Python, AI automation,
browser tooling, product UX, and end-to-end founder evidence. It has no stated
years floor in the current posting, pays $150,000 to $300,000 plus equity,
covers relocation, and exposes only four required application fields. It is not
certified S-tier because normal hours, nights and weekends, on-call, recovery,
the actual reporting line, and the reason the role is open remain unverified.

Do not use Sola's Windows AI Automation role as the primary target. That role
requires real .NET/C#, Win32, and COM depth that Adam does not yet have.

## Company problem and budget hypotheses

Treat these as hypotheses to test, not claims about Sola's private roadmap:

1. Enterprise users need automation creation to feel as direct and editable as
   a design tool while still producing reliable executions.
2. Browser and desktop surfaces change, so recorder output, selectors, retries,
   observability, and human handoff likely determine whether workflows survive
   production use.
3. Fortune 100 adoption likely creates pressure for permissions, auditability,
   session capture, and operational support alongside rapid product iteration.
4. The $150,000 to $300,000 band, covered relocation, funding, and simultaneous
   engineering hiring indicate a real approved budget for scarce product
   engineers, not a speculative talent-pool listing.

## Win mechanism

Lead with verified proof, not a generic claim of enthusiasm:

1. Show the five-case browser-application handoff evaluation and explain why a
   useful automation agent must stop at ambiguous identity, authentication, and
   final-submit boundaries.
2. Connect that reliability judgment to the public Helium Harness work and its
   Windows and browser-discovery tests.
3. Show that Adam can also own the surrounding product surface through
   skill.supply, not only the automation substrate.
4. Ask which failure mode currently creates the most customer pain: recording,
   workflow editing, runtime recovery, observability, or enterprise governance.

Do not publish the local proof or send it to anyone without Adam's deliberate
review and action.

## Required gates before certification

1. Who directly manages this Fullstack engineer?
2. What is one recent example of that person helping an engineer improve or
   broaden ownership?
3. What are normal weekly hours, and how many nights or weekends has the team
   worked in the last three months?
4. Is there an on-call or customer-incident rotation? What is its page frequency
   and response expectation?
5. What recovery follows a launch push or stressful incident?
6. Is the role new headcount, replacement, or a change in level?
7. Which product surface and metric would Adam own in the first 90 days?
8. What base, equity, and relocation package maps to Adam's evidence and level?

Reject S-tier certification if the reporting line stays unknown, recurring
overwork is normalized, recovery is absent, or the role is actually constrained
to a seniority level unsupported by Adam's experience.
