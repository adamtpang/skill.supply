## Select a role only when

- The posting is live, and its exact location, work authorization, and seniority
  do not rule Adam out. US citizenship and current UTC+8 location are facts, not
  a claim that every role permits remote work.
- Agent systems, applied AI, developer tools, or product engineering is central
  to the work. Generic roles receive a lower priority.
- At least two defensible proof links map directly to the JD: `skill.supply`,
  `megawatt.fun`, the agent-verification reconciler and its 12-scenario eval
  suite, or relevant end-to-end client delivery.
- The experience requirement is plausible for 2-3 years of engineering. A
  senior title is not disqualifying by itself, but its tenure gap must be named,
  not hidden.
- The application channel is explicit and the form can be completed truthfully.
  Do not infer answers to eligibility, compensation, demographics, or other
  sensitive questions.

Reject or defer a role if its page is closed, the location/visa constraint is
incompatible, the core work is unrelated, or the proof case depends on an
unverifiable claim.
