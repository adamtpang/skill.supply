## Workload and culture risk

The posting explicitly includes both on-call and first-responder rotations.
Frequency, page rate, response expectations, and recovery are not published.

GitHub's company-wide engineering account describes moving away from a
hero-based 24-hour monolith rotation toward team-owned rotations, training,
better runbooks, and balanced expertise. Its SERVICEOWNERS system gives incident
commanders explicit routes to the team responsible for each service. These are
positive system-design signals, but they are not current Secret Protection page
frequency or recovery evidence.

Company-wide anonymous evidence is mixed and cannot be projected onto this
team. Current reviews include positive work-life-balance and engineering-culture
reports, alongside reports of team-dependent management, US-hour concentration,
parallel-task load, understaffing, weak training, and burnout risk. This is why
People stops at 88 and the role remains `investigate`.

## Product research that strengthens the interview case

Secret Scanning is an active platform rather than a maintenance-only surface:

- [GitHub Secret Scanning documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/concepts/secret-security/secret-scanning) describes scanning across repository history, pull requests, issues, discussions, wikis, and gists, with partner notifications and push protection.
- [Copilot secret scanning engineering account](https://github.blog/engineering/platform-security/finding-leaked-passwords-with-ai-how-we-built-copilot-secret-scanning/) documents evaluation, mirror testing, private-preview feedback, and false-positive reduction.
- [Secret scanning through GitHub MCP Server](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-05-05-secret-scanning-with-github-mcp-server-is-now-generally-available/) became generally available in 2026.
- [Enterprise public monitoring](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-01-secret-scanning-public-monitoring-for-enterprises/) and [API, webhook, and delegated-workflow improvements](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-04-08-secret-scanning-improvements-to-alert-apis-webhooks-and-delegated-workflows/) show current expansion in alert operations and response workflows.

Adam's best interview bridge is the same engineering problem at a smaller scale:
how to reduce false actions while preserving useful automation and an auditable
handoff.
