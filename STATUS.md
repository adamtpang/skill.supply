## Perfect version

Skill Supply is the human-approved career agent that finds the best opportunity a person can credibly win, packages their real proof, queues the strongest shots, and learns from outcomes while the candidate performs every external application and communication action.

### Company outcome

- **Buyer or user:** Job seekers and career changers with strong but messy evidence; companies pay through darktalent.tech, not here.
- **Painful problem:** Capable people apply broadly with generic stories and cannot tell which company, team, role, offer, and location truly fit.
- **Transformation:** A messy background becomes a truthful career case, five high-quality targets, tailored proof, and a human-executed application sequence.
- **Proof that would make this undeniable:** One candidate manually submits a qualified application built by the system and reaches an interview or offer.
