Gumloop's handbook says applicants set themselves apart by trying the product
thoroughly. Do not send a generic founder note. First create truthful product
evidence with a bounded test called `S-tier role evidence gate`:

1. Give Gumloop one official role URL and a public-safe subset of Adam's profile.
2. Extract each role requirement into `supported`, `gap`, or `ask Adam`.
3. Require an evidence link or explicit candidate confirmation for every
   `supported` result.
4. Refuse to draft outreach or application language while required facts are
   missing.
5. Record the actual build time, output quality, failure modes, and one observed

6. Keep private contact information and local paths out of any shareable result.

the exact enterprise-agent control problem Gumloop sells. It must be performed
before any claim that Adam uses or likes Gumloop.
