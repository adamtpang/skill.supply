Prepared 2026-08-29 from current official role feeds, public team work, and the
campaign packets in this repository. Nothing has been sent or submitted.

## Decision

Browserbase Agent Platform is the first target to calibrate. It is the only
current investigation whose problem, hiring budget, people, and location scores
all reach 85. It remains `investigate`, not `s-tier`, because the direct manager,
normal hours, on-call and incident load, and recovery are still unknown.

Beacon Frontend/Web App is second. It is the closest clean match to Adam's
verified experience, but Beacon is simultaneously hiring the Lead Frontend
Engineer who is expected to mentor engineers and drive standards. The current
technical owner and coaching environment therefore cannot be assumed.

LangChain Frontend for the AI Observability and Evals Platform is third. The
live role's 2-year floor, current team contact routes, and LangSmith engineer
work align unusually well with Adam's evidence. The current manager and
sustainable operating cadence still cannot be assumed.

## Receipt to record from any reply or interview

Record exact words or a faithful factual summary. Do not infer a pass from tone.

| Field | Required evidence | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct manager | Name and actual reporting relationship | The manager is identified before offer acceptance |
| Coaching | One recent example involving a current engineer | Concrete feedback, sponsorship, expanded ownership, or deliberate skill development |
| Normal hours | Typical weekly range outside exceptional launches | Compatible with Adam's sustainable-work requirement |
| After-hours work | Frequency of nights and weekends in the last three months | Exceptional and bounded, not a recurring default |
| On-call and incidents | Rotation, page frequency, and expected response | Predictable load with clear ownership |
| Recovery | What follows an exceptional push or incident | Real compensatory recovery, not only unlimited PTO language |
| Role origin | Growth, replacement, or new direction | No hidden repeated attrition pattern |
| Offer | Level, base, equity, relocation support, and start timing | Supports savings and the required move |

## Scoring rule

- Keep `investigate` if the manager, workload, on-call, or recovery answer is
  missing.
- Reject S-tier status if recurring uncontrolled nights or weekends are normal,
  if the manager cannot show current coaching, or if the reporting line remains
  hidden through the interview loop.
- Certify Browserbase only if every required unknown is answered and no hard
  constraint fails. Its current numerical scores are already high enough, but
  the evidence packet is not complete.
- Do not raise Beacon above 84 while the effective frontend mentor and direct
  manager remain unknown.
- Do not raise LangChain above 84 from peer work, an adjacent manager promotion,
  or one current anonymous review alone.
