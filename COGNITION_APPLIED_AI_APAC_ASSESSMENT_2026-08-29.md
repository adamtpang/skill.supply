# Cognition Applied AI Engineer, APAC assessment

Checked 2026-08-29 against Cognition's official Ashby feed, current careers
page, current Singapore expansion announcement, company funding and usage
evidence, public APAC leadership material, and Adam's canonical profile.

## Verdict

Reject [Applied AI Engineer, APAC](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/cognition/12250aa8-c371-440c-8189-04872fd43eeb)
from Adam's S-tier campaign. Keep it only as an explicit high-compensation
income override if Adam knowingly accepts the workload culture.

The problem, budget, geography, and commercial-technical fit are unusually
strong. They do not compensate for the people-axis failure. TechCrunch reported
from a Cognition CEO email that remaining Windsurf employees were expected to
work six days in the office and more than 80 hours per week, alongside an
explicit rejection of work-life balance. Axios independently described the
same six-day, 80-hour expectation as Cognition's work-first ethos. The signal
comes from company leadership and was presented as a culture choice, not as one
anonymous review or one temporary launch.

The exact Singapore Applied AI schedule is not public, so it could be better.
That uncertainty cannot restore the score. Adam's S-tier contract requires
positive evidence of high standards without normalized abuse or chaos. Until
the exact team proves it is materially exempt, Cognition fails that gate.

## Income-override win mechanism

Use this only if Adam deliberately prioritizes near-term income, compensation,
or the mission over the S-tier people requirement. Lead with a deployment
thesis, not a generic coding-agent demo:

> Agent adoption becomes durable when one real workflow has a baseline, a
> ground-truth test set, explicit human-review boundaries, and a metric the
> engineering team trusts.

Use three existing proofs:

1. skill.supply for deploying a real agent product across live external data.
2. the summon.company register-truth evaluation for acceptance criteria and
   failure boundaries.
3. the Guam Power Authority workflow result as a carefully bounded example of
   measurable automation impact.

Do not claim enterprise adoption. Offer a simple thirty-day adoption scorecard
as a discussion artifact and invite Cognition to challenge its assumptions.
Do not apply or initiate outreach until Adam explicitly accepts the reported
six-day, 80-plus-hour culture risk.
