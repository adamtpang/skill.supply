# Replit Developer Experience assessment

Checked 2026-08-29 against the live role, Replit's current operating principles,
its current candidate self-selection page, and Adam's verified profile.

## Verdict

Reject [Software Engineer, Developer Experience](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Replit/d0e0dd7d-59d1-4de8-afbb-54aea680b51d)
from the S-tier campaign.

The problem, compensation, location, and technical overlap are strong. The
people gate fails on first-party company evidence, not rumor. Replit's operating
principles were updated in March 2026 and say that rapid change may burn people
out, that the company would rather win than protect people's emotions, and that
critical work can become an all-nighter. Its candidate self-selection page also
says Replit is not a 9-to-5 job and lacks a well-defined career ladder. Those
statements make unsustainable intensity an endorsed operating model rather than
an isolated incident to clarify later.

## Candidate fit

Adam has credible TypeScript, agent-tooling, browser-harness, testing,
documentation, packaging, and open-source evidence. The role has no stated
degree or years-of-experience floor. The honest gaps are material but potentially
bridgeable: no approved evidence establishes ownership of a large-company
monorepo, Nix, Go production depth, protobuf or gRPC code generation, or a
developer platform serving many internal engineering teams.

These gaps do not cause the rejection. The operating model does.

## Decision rule

Do not apply as part of Adam's S-tier campaign. Reconsider only if Replit
publicly replaces the current operating principles and the exact team supplies
strong evidence of sustainable hours, recovery after incidents, and manager-led
development. A private assurance cannot erase a company-wide first-party policy.
