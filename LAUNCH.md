# skill.supply: launch and market plan

Positioning: **You're the supply. We make you irresistible.** A free AI career agent that
turns anyone into a person companies fight to hire. Market it, never push it.

## The wedge (free, viral by design)
1. **The free report is the whole product and the whole ad.** Paste your background, get
   your ikigai, a packaged resume, five well-paid targets, five intros, five get-in plans,
   as an exportable kit. No skill.supply auth, no paywall, ever. **Changed 2026-08-06:**
   the seeker now pastes their own Anthropic API key too (bring-your-own-key, see OFFER.md),
   so skill.supply never carries the AI bill. This is a real friction addition worth
   watching in the share-loop numbers, not a free lunch.
2. **The share link is the growth loop.** The report lives in the URL hash (`/s#...`). When
   someone shares "here's the person I am: I'm the one who ___", their network sees a real,
   flattering artifact and wants their own. Every user is a billboard.
3. **The one-liner is the hook.** "You are the person who ___" is inherently screenshot-
   and-post shaped. Make it sharp enough to screenshot.

## Who pays (so "free" is a strategy, not charity)
- **Seekers stay free forever** (they are the supply, and per the no-sell-to-NS rule you
  never charge the network). The talent pool IS the asset.
- **Companies pay for the demand side**: access to scored, packaged, ready talent. This is
  the Moneyball model, and it pairs with darktalent.tech. skill.supply builds the supply;
  the demand side monetizes it. Do not bolt payments onto seekers.

## Channels (in priority order)
1. **The share loop first**: make sharing the report a one-tap, beautiful moment. This is
   the channel. Optimize the OG image (already have `opengraph-image.tsx`) so a shared link
   looks irresistible in a feed.
2. **Seed where seekers gather**: r/jobs, r/cscareerquestions, r/careerguidance, LinkedIn,
   X. Post "I built a free AI career agent that makes you irresistible to employers, here's
   mine" with your own report as the demo.
3. **The NS as free supply**: give it to the network for free (giving, not selling). They
   are early, high-signal users who share widely.
4. **Product Hunt launch**: "Free AI career agent, no signup, you own the output."
5. **Company-side outreach later**: once the talent pool is real, that is darktalent's job.

## First 100 users
Job-seekers in a painful search (new grads, career-switchers, laid-off tech workers). The
free exportable kit that gets them five applications out the door the same day is the value
they tell friends about.

## 14-day launch sequence
- Days 1-3: ship the placement loop (five intros + get-in plans + comp + export kit).
- Days 4-5: perfect the share moment and the OG card; run your own report as the demo.
- Days 6-8: seed 5 subreddits + an X thread, each with a real report link.
- Day 9: Product Hunt launch, "no signup, own your output."
- Days 10-14: collect the best user one-liners, repost them (with permission) as proof;
  watch the share loop compound.

## The one metric
Reports shared (the `/s#` links opened by someone other than their creator). That is the
viral coefficient, and it predicts everything downstream, including the demand side.
